About

I work across three fields: robotics education, cybersecurity and networks, and finance. Since 2023 I have trained and led Team Kenya at the FIRST Global Challenge — first as team captain in Singapore, then as technical mentor in Athens, and as chief technical trainer in Panama, where the team finished second in the world.

I coach students from middle school through IB Diploma level in robot build, programming, and competition strategy. Outside robotics I have worked as a network analyst at Konvergenz Network Solutions, trained AI models on contract, and spent a season advising clients on insurance and savings at Britam.
